# Internal Review

The CEO and board have a few minutes per delivery unit. They want to know: are we hitting targets, where is growth coming from, what is at risk, and what do you need from us. The deck gives the portfolio view first and account detail second, with every long table in the appendix. It is candid, because this is the room where problems get solved.

## Workflow

1. Collect inputs, then research the gaps (SKILL.md, step 3). Ask for the account tracker (FTE, revenue, targets, pipeline, feedback) if it exists. If not, gather the essentials per account in one round and use placeholders.
2. Decide the 3 messages leadership must leave with. They drive the executive summary.
3. Draft the storyline with conclusion titles and confirm.
4. Build, then validate.

## Inputs

| Input | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and delivery unit | Free text | Cover |
| Targets | FTE, revenue, growth, per account and total | Slides 2 and 3 |
| Actuals | Same measures, current and last period | Slides 2 and 3 |
| Pipeline | Opportunities with stage, value, owner | Slide 4 |
| Wins and misses | Free text | Slide 5 |
| People data | Headcount, attrition, bench, open positions, client feedback scores | Slide 6 |
| Risks and escalations | Account-level and unit-level | Slide 7 |

## Storyline (cap 8 plus 1 per account, tolerance 2)

| # | Slide | Leadership question | Hero visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover | Which unit and period? | Cover |
| 2 | Executive summary | How are we doing, and what do you need? | Scorecard of 4 to 6 headline measures against target, 3 key messages, and the decisions needed |
| 3 | Portfolio health | Which accounts are on track? | Heatmap: accounts by FTE versus target, growth, delivery health, relationship health, outlook |
| 4 | Growth | Where will growth come from? | Pipeline funnel with value by stage, top opportunities, and conversion |
| 5 | What worked and what did not | What did we learn? | Split panel with 3 to 4 items each and the action taken |
| 6 | People and delivery health | Is the team strong and stable? | Scorecard: headcount, attrition, bench, open positions and time to fill, client feedback average |
| 7 | Risks and escalations | What could hurt us? | Top 5 risks with impact, mitigation, and help needed from leadership |
| 8 | Next quarter plan and asks | What is the plan, and what do you need from us? | Targets by account, 3 priorities, and an asks strip (hiring, investment, executive sponsorship, decisions) |
| 9+ | Account pages, 1 per account | What is happening in each account? | Account one-pager (below) |
| A | Appendix | Detail | Outreach and contact lists, stakeholder connects, resource-level feedback, detailed pipeline, farming and mining plans, email templates |

### Account one-pager

One slide per account, same layout every time so leadership can compare:

- **Header strip**: account name, owner, FTE actual versus target, trend arrow, overall health RAG.
- **Left column**: what happened since the last review (3 bullets), relationship and feedback summary with average score.
- **Middle column**: growth, meaning top 3 opportunities with stage and value, and key stakeholder connects.
- **Right column**: risks, and the plan for the next 4 weeks with owners.

### Slide notes

- **Executive summary**: write it last. The 3 messages are conclusions ("Two accounts drive 80% of growth; Account C needs executive sponsorship"). Decisions needed are specific and dated.
- **Portfolio health**: numbers in cells, RAG as light fill. Sort accounts by size.
- **Growth**: distinguish farming (grow existing work) from mining (new stakeholders and divisions) with a tag.
- **What worked and what did not**: honest and specific. Each "did not work" item has the action taken.
- **People and delivery health**: resource-level feedback never appears by name on main slides. Show averages and themes; names stay in the appendix for leadership only.
- **Next quarter plan and asks**: every ask has an owner in leadership and a date.

## Flex rules

- One or two accounts only: merge slide 3 into slide 2 and skip account pages; put the account detail on slides 4 to 7 directly.
- Many accounts (more than 6): account pages cover the top 6 by size or risk; the rest get one combined slide.
- Past-quarter recap only (no forward plan requested): keep slide 8 as "What we will do next quarter" in brief. Leadership always expects a forward view.
- Farming and mining plans, outreach lists, and email templates are always appendix material.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The executive summary stands alone with headline numbers against target, 3 messages, and dated decisions.
- [ ] Every account appears on the portfolio heatmap with the same measures.
- [ ] Account pages use the identical layout.
- [ ] Long tables (contacts, outreach, individual feedback) are in the appendix, not the main flow.
- [ ] Every risk has a mitigation and states whether leadership help is needed.
- [ ] Asks have owners and dates.
