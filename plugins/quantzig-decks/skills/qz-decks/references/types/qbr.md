# QBR

The QBR shows the client what the partnership achieved this quarter in business terms, agrees priorities for next quarter, and plants one growth idea. It is a conversation more than a report: feedback is gathered in the room, so the deck leaves space for it. Operational detail stays in the MBR.

## Workflow

1. Collect inputs, then research the gaps (SKILL.md, step 3). Pull from the quarter's MBRs, trackers, delivered features, and last quarter's QBR commitments.
2. Choose one growth idea and its form (use case extension, case study proof, or point of view). See the Growth pitch block in `references/blocks.md`.
3. Draft the storyline with conclusion titles and confirm.
4. Build, then validate.

## Inputs

| Input | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarter, account, and stakeholder team | Free text | Cover |
| Partnership footprint | Teams, workstreams, FTEs, years | Slide 3 |
| Goals set last quarter | List, or none recorded | Slide 4 |
| Value delivered | Outcomes with numbers and basis | Slides 2 and 4 |
| Key deliveries | Features, releases, improvements | Slide 5 |
| Growth idea | Use case extension / Case study proof / Point of view / None this quarter | Slide 6 |
| Next quarter priorities | List | Slide 7 |
| Known feedback | From MBRs, emails, stakeholder conversations | Slide 8 |

## Storyline (cap 8, tolerance 1)

| # | Slide | Client question | Hero visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover | Which quarter and team? | Cover with the quarter's headline as subtitle |
| 2 | Quarter at a glance | Was this quarter worth it? | Value strip: value delivered, highlights, what is next, how you can help |
| 3 | Our partnership | Where do you work with us? | Partnership map with 3 or 4 footprint numbers |
| 4 | Value delivered | What did it do for the business? | KPI tiles or before-after bars against last quarter's goals |
| 5 | What we delivered | What changed in the platform or process? | 4 to 6 delivery cards plus an in-progress strip |
| 6 | An idea worth exploring | What else could we do? | Growth pitch block, 1 slide (2 when a sample output helps) |
| 7 | Looking ahead | What will we focus on next quarter? | Now, Next, Later columns or three columns: what is working, what we commit to, what to explore |
| 8 | Your feedback and next steps | What matters most to you? | Feedback prompt cards plus a next-steps strip |
| A | Appendix | Detail | Workstream detail, full ideas list, metric definitions, delivery log |

### Slide notes

- **Quarter at a glance**: the value tile carries 2 or 3 business numbers. The last tile asks for something specific (access, a sponsor for the idea, a priority call).
- **Our partnership**: keep it short for a stakeholder team that knows us well. For a new stakeholder, this slide matters more.
- **Value delivered**: map each outcome to a goal set last quarter. If no goals were recorded, set them on slide 7 so next QBR can close the loop. Business terms first ("one model now replaces 50", "code coverage 0% to 84%, so releases are safer").
- **An idea worth exploring**: one idea, tied to something observed in their business this quarter. No pricing. The ask is a follow-up conversation, a demo, or a short discovery.
- **Looking ahead**: include commitments from Quantzig, not only asks of the client.
- **Your feedback and next steps**: prompts invite conversation. If earlier feedback exists, show "You said, we did" pairs.

## Flex rules

- Several stakeholder teams in one QBR: slide 3 maps all teams, and slides 4 and 5 group by team with the top 2 or 3 teams in the main flow and the rest in the appendix.
- Tough quarter (missed goals or escalations): slide 4 states it plainly, and slide 7 leads with the recovery commitments. Drop the growth idea unless the client asks.
- Two strong growth ideas: keep one in the main flow and list the other in the appendix ideas slide.
- Small or short QBR: merge slides 4 and 5, and merge 7 and 8, for a 6-slide deck.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Value is stated in business terms with numbers and basis, not as a list of activities.
- [ ] Last quarter's goals or commitments are closed out, or next quarter's goals are set.
- [ ] Exactly one growth idea in the main flow, tied to an observed need, with no pricing.
- [ ] The deck leaves room for feedback and ends with next steps, owners, and dates.
- [ ] No internal metrics (margins, rates, utilization) in this client-facing deck.
