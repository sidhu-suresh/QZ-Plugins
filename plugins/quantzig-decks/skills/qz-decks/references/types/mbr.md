# MBR

The MBR is the client's monthly view of delivery health: are we on track, what did we deliver, what is at risk, and what do we need from them. It is operational and short. Value stories and growth pitches belong to the QBR, so the MBR stays focused and quick to run every month.

## Workflow

1. Collect inputs, then research the gaps (SKILL.md, step 3). Ask for the team's Excel tracker if it exists (milestones, tickets, FTE, RAID). If not, gather the essentials in one round of questions and use placeholders for the rest.
2. For a repeat MBR, reuse last month's deck as the base: keep structure, update numbers, roll dates forward, and compare to last month.
3. Draft the storyline with conclusion titles and confirm.
4. Build, then validate.

## Inputs

| Input | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Month and account or program | Free text | Cover |
| Workstreams in scope | List | Slides 3 and 4 |
| Overall and per-workstream status | Green / Amber / Red, with reason | Slides 2 and 3 |
| Delivery KPIs | Milestones on time, SLA adherence, tickets or defects, release count, uptime, anything the client tracks | Slide 3 |
| Delivered this month and planned next month | Per workstream | Slide 4 |
| Risks, issues, decisions needed | RAID items | Slide 5 |
| Team changes | Joiners, exits, backfills, open positions | Slide 6 |
| Tracker file | Excel available / Not available | Whether numbers are filled or marked as placeholders |

## Storyline (cap 6, tolerance 1)

| # | Slide | Client question | Hero visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover | Which month and account? | Cover with the month's headline as subtitle |
| 2 | Month at a glance | Are we OK, and what do you need from me? | Status banner plus status strip: overall status, highlights, watch-outs, decisions needed |
| 3 | Delivery scorecard | How healthy is delivery? | Scorecard of KPIs against target with trend versus last month, plus workstream RAG heatmap |
| 4 | Delivered and next | What did we get, and what is coming? | This month and next month split by workstream |
| 5 | Risks, issues, and decisions | What could go wrong? | RAID table, top 6, with decisions needed highlighted |
| 6 | Team and capacity | Is the team stable? | Team strip with FTE by workstream, changes this month, and open positions with dates |
| A | Appendix | Detail | Ticket or defect detail, full RAID, milestone plan, workstream detail pages |

### Slide notes

- **Month at a glance**: the overall status has a one-line reason. Highlights are outcomes ("inventory report now refreshes daily"), not activity ("worked on the report").
- **Delivery scorecard**: use the KPIs the client already tracks. Show the trend against last month. Keep to 8 rows.
- **Delivered and next**: next month's plan becomes next MBR's "delivered" list. Keep item names identical across months so progress is traceable.
- **Risks, issues, and decisions**: follow the RAID block. Decisions needed from the client appear first and are repeated on slide 2.
- **Team and capacity**: never show rates or costs. Show capacity changes and how gaps are covered.

## Flex rules

- Single-workstream account: drop the heatmap from slide 3 and use the full slide for the scorecard.
- Many workstreams (more than 5): slide 4 covers the top 5 by client priority; the rest go to one appendix slide each.
- An agreed improvement idea or small enhancement to raise: add one Growth pitch slide (`references/blocks.md`, section 2) after slide 6. Do not add more than one.
- Escalation month (overall Red): slide 2 leads with the recovery plan, and add a recovery plan slide after slide 5 with actions, owners, and dates.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Overall and workstream statuses follow the RAG definitions and each Amber or Red has a reason and action.
- [ ] Every KPI has a target and a comparison with last month.
- [ ] Last month's "next month" items are accounted for in this month's delivered list or explained.
- [ ] Decisions needed from the client appear on slides 2 and 5 with owners and dates.
- [ ] No rates, margins, or internal commentary in this client-facing deck.
- [ ] Six slides or seven with a growth or recovery slide. Everything else is in the appendix.
