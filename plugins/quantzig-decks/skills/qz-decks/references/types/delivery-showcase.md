# Delivery Showcase

A delivery showcase tells client leadership what a phase achieved, what it means for their business, and what the next phase should be. Leaders did not see the day-to-day work, so the deck leads with numbers and decisions, keeps method in the appendix, and always ends with next steps.

## Workflow

1. Collect inputs, then research the gaps (SKILL.md, step 3). Pull from phase deliverables, analysis outputs, dashboards, and the original phase plan.
2. Pick the 3 insights that matter most to leadership. Everything else goes to the appendix.
3. Build the value logic: how the opportunity or savings number adds up, driver by driver, with basis.
4. Draft the storyline with conclusion titles and confirm.
5. Build, then validate.

## Inputs

| Input | Options | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase objective and original plan | Free text or earlier deck | Slide 3 |
| What was delivered | Models, dashboards, analyses, simulators, data assets | Slides 2 and 4 |
| Top insights | 3 at most, each with evidence | Slides 5 to 7 |
| Value or opportunity sized | Number, drivers, basis | Slides 2 and 8 |
| Adoption so far | Users, decisions made, process changes | Slide 9 |
| Open issues | Data gaps, dependencies, risks | Slide 10 |
| Next phase | Scope, duration, value, team | Slide 11 |
| Audience | CXO / Functional leadership / Mixed | Depth of slides 5 to 7 |

## Storyline (cap 12, tolerance 2)

| # | Slide | Leadership question | Hero visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover | What is this? | Cover with the phase outcome as title |
| 2 | Phase in numbers | What did we get? | 3 or 4 big-number KPI tiles plus a key wins strip |
| 3 | Where we started | What did we set out to do? | Chevron journey of all phases with this phase highlighted and its objective banner |
| 4 | What we delivered | What exists now? | Capability map of delivered modules, each with one-line purpose |
| 5 to 7 | Insight 1 to 3 | What did we learn about the business? | One per slide: the chart or example that proves it, with a so-what box |
| 8 | The value | How big is the prize? | Waterfall from baseline to opportunity, each driver labelled with basis |
| 9 | What changed | Is anyone using it? | Before-after split of the process, plus adoption numbers |
| 10 | What needs attention | What could hold us back? | Three cards: data, process, and people risks, each with the action needed |
| 11 | The next phase | What should we do next? | Swimlane of the next phase with scope, deliverables, duration, and expected value |
| 12 | Next steps and decisions | What do you need from us? | Next-steps timeline ending in a go decision for the next phase |
| A | Appendix | How did you do it? | Detailed method, datasets, segmentation logic, business questions answered, module deep dives |

### Slide notes

- **Phase in numbers**: combine outcome numbers (opportunity sized, savings) with scale numbers (sites covered, models built). Outcome numbers go first.
- **Insight slides**: title states the insight ("Elite Spenders Drive 40% of Revenue From 12% of Games"). One chart or example, one so-what box: what the business should do about it.
- **The value**: show the opportunity as a range if estimated. State which levers are within the client's control.
- **What changed**: be specific about decisions made with the output ("pricing changed on 30 machines across 4 sites").
- **The next phase**: this is the growth pitch. It follows naturally from the insights and open issues. Tie each next-phase item to an insight or issue from earlier slides.

## Flex rules

- More than 3 insights: keep 3 in the main flow and put the rest in the appendix as one slide each.
- Many modules (segmentation, association mining, simulator, profitability): slide 4 shows them as a capability map; module deep dives go to the appendix.
- No next phase agreed yet: slide 11 shows 2 or 3 options with value and effort, and slide 12 asks for a choice.
- Program with multiple scenarios (for example revenue uplift scenarios): show the base case on slide 8 and the scenarios in the appendix.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Leadership can read slides 2, 8, 11, and 12 alone and know the result, the value, and the ask.
- [ ] At most 3 insights in the main flow. Method and module detail is in the appendix.
- [ ] The value number on slide 2 equals the waterfall total on slide 8.
- [ ] Every next-phase item links back to an insight or open issue.
- [ ] The deck ends with next steps, owners, dates, and a decision.
